# Detail v4 — XR / GLB only

2026-11-03. Presentation and private reference images were not edited or published.

## Design contract
CAD shell, columns and circulation remain authoritative. Oak/terrazzo/charcoal/felt are shared; orange emitted light identifies visitor action/contributions. Architectural lights and Arena ring remain warm white. Products are original illustrative procedural concepts, not adidas product specifications.

## Changes
- Three footwear construction families: tapered uppers, collar openings, heel panels, lacing, separate midsoles/outsoles and trail lugs. One active Lab demonstration at a time, material trays and neutral construction diagram. Construction layers and labels appear only during activation/showcase export.
- Framed Imprint backing, smoother eight-sided round caps at the same triangle cost by omitting concealed rear caps, fuller silhouettes, coherent relief depth and grazing light. 80 mm pitch retained to preserve the mobile triangle budget; no real stride sensing.
- Culture-night host mode: original procedural Bengaluru movement artwork, DJ decks and speakers, warm-white ring. Everyday mode shows a conceptual retractable-tier storage envelope. Run mats appear only in run-club mode. Storage/retraction mechanism remains a design proposal.
- Workshop: cutting mats, shoe blanks, knit/foam/rubber samples, pigments, brushes, simple tools and material-study pin-ups. Studio: acoustic strips and a deliberate recording backdrop. Quiet rooms retain prior upholstery and lighting.
- Lower contrast/finer procedural terrazzo; no new external assets, dependencies or backend. No physical boundaries moved.
