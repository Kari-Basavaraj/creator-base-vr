# Experience v3: intent, brief alignment and prototype evidence

This is the optional interactive prototype for a hypothetical Zyeta experience-design assignment. It does not replace the assignment's required 16:9 PDF of at most 20 slides. No original assignment PDF, CAD or private pitch materials are republished here.

## 1. Insight and principles

**Working insight, not a validated research finding:** a premium brand space can feel like a place for other people. Participation can make a first-time visitor feel they have a place in the story.

**Leave your mark:** effort becomes a story we share.

Five principles: belong before you perform; touch to understand; make effort visible; protect calm; give people a reason to return. These are the proposal's design choices, not claims that the assignment prescribed them.

## 2. One connected story

Arrival compresses through smoked oak, then opens to the north glass. A visitor crossing the start line wakes a light trace. The Lab opens a product into a construction story. A visitor relief is the peak; Fuel and Recovery provide resolution. The external sky/time is shared across the floor, while local light, material, acoustic character and activity vary by zone.

The performance-science layer lives in construction and comparison; the culture layer in replaceable printed community pin-up and city orientation; the participation layer in the maker wall and visitor relief. Artwork and product forms are concept placeholders, not real adidas collaborations or specifications.

## 4. Zoning, route and calm

- West: Arena, making and production. East: focused work and partner decisions. The north band connects the arrival, Lab, welcome and recovery spaces.
- The main visitor route has 428 valid centre-line samples against the current collision model. This is not an accessibility or egress certification.
- The northern promenade is moved south of the perimeter-column obstructions. Quiet neutral inlays carry the route down the Fuel/Recovery edge and into the Arena. Orange stops before calm areas.
- The westward glimpse is taken along the northern promenade through the northwest glazing recess; the previous claim of an unobstructed straight y=40.4 sightline is not retained.
- Fuel and Recovery require no interaction. Team Base has a felt-floor treatment and quieter light/sound levels. The studio is acoustically suggested, not acoustically engineered.
- The core-side staff interface is now traversable. A small service recess is explicitly an assumption; full core circulation still needs the original CAD.
- The stage is 5.0 × 2.6 m, clear of the fixed column footprint. Six tiers contain 84 audience cushions, with two proposed step-free floor positions. Occupied sightlines, aisle design, capacity, fire strategy and accessibility still need professional validation.

## 5. Three signature moments: 0 / 5 / 20 seconds

| Moment | Trigger / medium | 0 seconds | 5 seconds | 20 seconds | Intended outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start line | Visitor crosses the floor line; architectural light and material | Neutral standby changes to visitor orange | The trace advances around the left turn | The active route is legible; the northward reveal is open | Move forward with anticipation, without a presentation |
| Lift to learn | E/click, hand/controller proximity or controller ray acts on a physical-product proxy; 3D layers and interpretation cards | A selected shoe starts lifting | Construction layers separate and explain their jobs | Intended use, trade-offs and evidence/care questions appear; shortlist available | Understand a product by interacting with it |
| Imprint | Visitor steps on the pad; screen-free instanced pin field | Athlete relief is present; visitor field begins responding | Visitor relief has risen over a four-second animation | The relief remains; local history and a take-home image are available | Recognize participation and create a reason to return |

The Imprint is an **illustrative, generated visit mark**, not motion capture, pressure-pad hardware or a measured stride. Its depth pattern varies per saved local mark. No body or biometric data is captured.

## 6. Make one moment real: Lift to Learn

1. Standby: shoe on its oak block, no orange halo and no story card.
2. Input: a nearby keyboard/click action, controller proximity, or an aimed controller trigger selects the product proxy. The desktop action chooses the nearest shoe, not the first shoe in the array.
3. Response: the shoe rises; the upper/midsole/outsole separate. Cards progress at 0, 5 and 20 seconds.
4. Outcome: the visitor can shortlist the concept, compare its purpose/construction with another concept and export a plain-text note.
5. Reset: 30-second timeout or host reset returns to standby. Seasonal content is replaced from a small data bundle without moving furniture.
6. Fallback: keyboard/click and HTML explanations remain available when immersive controllers or audio are unavailable. Storage failures are disclosed; persistence is not silently promised.
7. First physical test: use a weighted product dummy, a simple lift sensor or observer trigger, timed light/content cues and two people. Observe whether the story is understood without a host pitch. This physical/user test has **not** been conducted.

## What changed after failures and audits

- Branding existed locally but had not reached the original public snapshot. It is now in the repo, visible at entry, and verified through GLB re-import.
- The staff-door label was not evidence of passage. The core/walkable-area interface is now explicitly cut and checked at approach, threshold and inside points.
- Four tiers/52 cushions did not demonstrate the brief's audience range. Six tiers/84 modeled seats are a proposal within that range; compliance is not claimed.
- The old stage intersected a column. Its footprint is reduced and shifted clear, while fixed columns remain unchanged.
- The old route clipped a perimeter corner and squeezed the column line. Its sampled route now avoids those conflicts.
- Rest pins were hidden in the south partition. Their mounting origin and an off-white backing field are corrected, and the actual exported file is rendered for inspection.
- Placeholder rooms now have recognizable tools, production equipment, a private comparison setup, café details, configurable content and host controls.

## Evidence versus remaining work

Browser checks, source checks and actual exported-model renders are under `validation/`. Human outcomes, journey durations, 80–120-person operation, occupied sightlines, accessibility and headset comfort/performance are untested. Browser storage is local to the origin/device: there is no shared cloud crew archive. The city/landmark remains assumed massing, not a georeferenced site model.

The final submission still needs the user's reviewed thinking in the required presentation PDF. Disclose AI use for source-code generation, conceptual copy, procedural geometry/materials and technical test orchestration. Local CC0 assets and official-site wordmark provenance are documented separately.
